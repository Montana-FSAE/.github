# Montana FSAE 🏁

Student-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit Formula SAE team at **Montana State University** in Bozeman, MT. We design, build, and race a formula-style car every season.

🌐 **[montanafsae.com](https://montanafsae.com)**

## Who we are

Founded in 2024, Montana FSAE gives MSU students hands-on experience turning classroom engineering into a real race car. Members work across aerodynamics, chassis, manufacturing, composites, electronics, business, and sponsorship — taking the car from concept to competition while building skills in design, project management, and teamwork.

As one of MSU's newest engineering organizations, we're building a tradition of innovation, technical excellence, and student leadership, and representing MSU on the national stage.

## About FSAE

Formula SAE is a collegiate design competition where university teams design, build, and race a formula-style vehicle, combining engineering, manufacturing, and business into one real-world motorsports project.

### Repo naming

`<prefix>-<project>` in lowercase-kebab-case, e.g. `esys-pdm-firmware`, `aero-cfd-scripts`.

### Getting started

1. Get added to the org and the **members** team by a lead (send your GitHub username).
2. Read [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/Montana-FSAE/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. Create repos for your projects — new repos are **private** by default; ask a lead to make one public.
